    Party Rest

    Choose a rest duration for all party tokens:

    Partial Rest (8 hours): Restores Mana and Stamina equal to level, Physical Armor equal to Fortitude score, Magical Armor equal to Intelligence score, and clears Tired.

    Full Rest (24 hours): Restores Health, Mana, Stamina, and all Armor pools to maximum, clears Exhausted and Dying, and refreshes per-full-rest features.

    Sparks Quick Reference

    6 AP Economy: Gain 6 AP at turn start (+1 Hastened, −1 Slowed, max 2 Freezing, 0 Paralyzed/Petrified/Stunned/Unconscious/Dying). Unspent AP is lost at turn end.
    Movement: 1 AP adds Speed meters to your Move Pool for the turn. Difficult terrain costs 2 m per 1 m. Hold Shift while dragging to reposition freely.
    Critical & Decisive Strikes: Physical attacks roll d100 ≤ Critical Chance (Wits score) for ×2 dice. A roll in the Decisive Range (20, widened by Deadly) is an automatic Critical and rolls d100 ≤ Crit Chance for Decisive (×3 dice + Crippled).
    Damage Order: Immunity → Vulnerability (×2) → Resistance (minus Enchanted ×5; Vorpal/Piercing ignore physical res/imm) → Armor (Physical → PA; Elemental/Psychic → MA; Holy vs Evil / Unholy vs Good / Magic vs Neutral / True → TA then HP; Monsters use single AV).
    Vision & POV: Normal needs light in darkness; Dark Sight sees through darkness; Blind Sight perceives within 10 m; True Vision pierces True Darkness & Invisibility.
